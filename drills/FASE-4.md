# Drill FASE 4 — Present Perfect: o molde de entrevista (semana 4)

> **Como usar:** prompt em português → **falar em inglês em voz alta** → conferir. 3 passadas
> por dia. Nesta fase você NÃO estuda a teoria completa do Present Perfect — decora **o molde**
> que abre resposta de entrevista, e treina a virada: **abre no Present Perfect, detalha no
> Past Simple.** É assim que um nativo responde "Tell me about your experience with X".

## Referência rápida

- Molde: **I've** + particípio → *I've worked with..., I've built..., I've led...*
- Particípios que você vai usar: worked, built, led, created, used, made, had, been, done, learned
- A virada: *I've built several systems.* **(geral)** → *For example, in 2024 I built...* **(específico, passado)**

## Drill A — o molde puro (12)

1. Eu tenho trabalhado com IA. → *I've worked with AI.*
2. Eu já construí sistemas em produção. → *I've built systems in production.*
3. Eu já liderei times. → *I've led teams.*
4. Eu já criei produtos do zero. → *I've created products from scratch.*
5. Eu tenho usado TypeScript e Python. → *I've used TypeScript and Python.*
6. Eu já tive minha própria empresa. → *I've had my own company.*
7. Eu tenho feito isso por doze anos. → *I've done this for twelve years.*
8. Eu já trabalhei com clientes grandes. → *I've worked with big clients.*
9. Eu tenho aprendido muito rápido. → *I've learned very fast.*
10. Eu já estive nessa situação antes. → *I've been in this situation before.*
11. Nós já lançamos três versões. → *We've launched three versions.*
12. Eu já fiz essa integração antes. → *I've made this integration before.*

## Drill B — a virada: geral → específico (8 pares)

Fale o par completo, sem parar entre as duas frases:

1. *I've built management systems.* → *For example, last year I built a complete booking platform.*
2. *I've worked with AI for two years.* → *In my last project, I used three AI agents in production.*
3. *I've led sales teams.* → *In 2015, I led a team of ten people.*
4. *I've created companies before.* → *I started my first company in 2012 and sold it in 2018.*
5. *I've used PostgreSQL a lot.* → *I wrote more than four hundred migrations for one system.*
6. *I've had experience with integrations.* → *I connected the system to more than two hundred channels.*
7. *I've done big projects alone.* → *I built the whole platform without a team.*
8. *I've learned new tools fast.* → *I learned Deno in two weeks and shipped with it.*

## Drill C — perguntas reais de entrevista (responda em voz alta com o molde)

Responda cada uma começando com *I've...* e emendando um exemplo no passado:

1. Tell me about your experience with AI.
2. Have you worked with TypeScript?
3. Have you ever led a team?
4. What kind of systems have you built?
5. Have you worked with databases?
6. Have you ever started a company?
7. What have you learned in the last year?
8. Have you worked remotely before?
9. Have you had experience with APIs?
10. Why have you decided to look for a new role?

## Drill D — o pitch de 60 segundos (montar e falar)

Junte tudo: fale este esqueleto em voz alta, preenchendo com a sua história — 1x devagar, 1x
no tempo, 1x cronometrado em 60s:

> *I've been a founder and engineer for twelve years. I've built and sold a company, and I've
> led sales and technical teams. In 2024, I started to build a complete management platform —
> I built it alone, with AI. The system went to production this year. Now I'm looking for a
> role where I can build products with AI every day.*

---

**Saída da fase:** responder 5 perguntas do Drill C seguidas, cada uma abrindo no Present
Perfect e detalhando no Past Simple, sem pausa longa. Aí: **Fase 5** — simulados completos
comigo ("treino de fala" avançado) + vocabulário técnico (fatias B1+).
